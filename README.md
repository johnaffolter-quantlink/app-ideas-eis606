# App Ideas — EIS 606 Homework 1

A list of app ideas I might "vibe code" (build quickly with AI-assisted coding tools, prototyping fast and iterating by feel rather than a full spec).

1. **Macro Pulse** — A lightweight dashboard that pulls a handful of public macro indicators (CPI, rates, PMI) and auto-generates a plain-English "what changed this week" digest, instead of just showing raw charts.

2. **Notebook-to-Slide** — Turns a data notebook (cells + charts) into a shareable slide deck in one click, while keeping each slide wired to its underlying query so the numbers stay live instead of going stale.

3. **Ontology Mapper** — Drag-and-drop columns from two messy CSVs/spreadsheets and get suggestions for how they map to a shared schema, useful for quickly reconciling data from different systems.

4. **Habit Ledger** — A personal finance + habit tracker that treats savings goals like "positions" you're building toward, with simple progress bars instead of spreadsheets.

5. **Meeting Memory** — Records a meeting and builds a searchable, linked map of decisions and open questions, rather than just producing a flat transcript.

6. **Recipe Remix** — Enter the ingredients you already have on hand and get recipes ranked by how few substitutions or extra trips to the store they'd require.

7. **Study Buddy** — Upload lecture slides or readings and automatically generate spaced-repetition flashcards for exam prep.

8. **Local Deals Radar** — Aggregates local events and discounts (Twin Cities area) into one weekly digest instead of checking five different sites.
